# Registering your PSN account 
:::info[Please read this section very, very carefully]
I keep getting questions and complaints from people who havent read the documentation. Please, read
:::

## 1. Set up your PlayStation console for remote play<Device kind="playstation" />

### a. Enable remote play

<Tabs groupId="console">
<TabItem value="ps5" label="PS5" default>

Go to `Settings -> System -> Remote Play` and turn on:

- `Enable Remote Play`

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="ps4" label="PS4">

1. Go to `Settings -> Remote Play Connection Settings` and turn on:
   - `Enable Remote Play`
2. Go to `Settings -> Account Management -> Activate as Your Primary PS4` and select:
   - `Activate`

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### b. Enable waking your console from rest mode

<Tabs groupId="console">
<TabItem value="ps5" label="PS5" default>

Go to `Settings -> System -> Power Saving -> Features Available in Rest Mode` and turn on:

- `Stay Connected to the Internet`
- `Enable Turning on PS5 from Network`

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="ps4" label="PS4">

Go to `Settings -> Power Save Settings -> Set Function Available in Rest Mode` and turn on:

- `Stay Connected to the Internet`
- `Enable turning on PS4 from Network`

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::note
Over PSN, PS4 remote connection only works with the primary console on your account. That's a Sony limitation, not ours.
:::

## 2. Log in to PSN in the companion app<Device kind="pc" />

:::note[Companion]
You only ever need to use the companion for registring an account.
:::

The companion app provides a simplified way of fetching tokens and transmitting them to the switch.

Because most of the userbase is incapable of performing this action manually, the companion app is the only way avaliable for first time users.

Grab the Akira Companion app from the release and run it.

On Linux, `chmod +x` it first, then run it.

:::note[macOS]
macOS blocks unknown publishers by default. Allow it under `System Settings -> Privacy & Security`. 
:::

![Companion sign-in (guided-paste) screen](/img/pair/01-companion-signin.png)

1. **Sign in to PlayStation.** Click **Open PlayStation.com**, sign in (top-right), come back.
2. **Open the NPSSO page.** Click **Open NPSSO page**. Now that you're signed in, it shows `{"npsso":"..."}`.

   ![Browser showing the npsso JSON](/img/pair/02-npsso-json.png)

3. **Paste the npsso token.** Copy the value between the quotes (drop the `"`), paste it in, click **Sign in**.


![Companion "Send to your Switch" screen](/img/pair/03-companion-send.png)

:::note[Unconnectable]
If you are unable to automatically discover your switch, please make sure that your switch and computer are on the same subnet, otherwise please enter the ip in manually.
:::

## 3. Open the pairing screen on the switch <Device kind="switch" />

On the switch, as a new user, you can pair through either the settings page or the "Set up your account" tab on your host page.

![pairing1](/img/pair/pairing2.jpg)
![pairing2](/img/pair/pairing1.jpg)

You will now see a pairing screen with your switch's ip address, port, and a 4-digit code.

![pairing3](/img/pair/pairing3.jpg)

Leave thi screen open until the pairing is complete. 

## 4. Pair with the switch from your desktop<Device kind="pc" />

Back on the computer, on the **Send to your Switch** screen:

- Same network? The Switch shows up under **Found on your network**. Click it to fill in the IP and port.
- Different subnet, or it didn't show up? Type the Switch IP and port from the Pair screen. Manual always works.

![pairing4](/img/pair/pairing4.png)
![pairing5](/img/pair/pairing5.png)

Enter the 4-digit code, then click **Send credentials**.

![pairing6](/img/pair/pairing6.png)

The companion now has sent the tokens to the switch, and you should be ready to proceed without the companion. Go back to the host screen on the switch

![pairing7](/img/pair/pairing7.jpg)

Your PSN account now lives on the switch. Next: [add and register a console](./connecting-your-first-host).
